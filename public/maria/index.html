01 · Typography

The five fonts

RecConnect has one loud voice and one quiet one. Anton, Reissue and Bebas Neue do the shouting on hero slides, social cards and section titles. Inter does all the reading. Space Grotesk is the reserve heading face for calmer pages.

None of them need stretching, squashing or faking. Anton and Bebas Neue are condensed by design, so set them at their natural width and let the letterforms do the work.

Anton

Headlines. The big condensed capitals.

Use for
Hero titles, section headings, big statistics (200+, 2,500+), social card titles.
Case
Always capitals. Anton in lower case is not RecConnect.
Weight
One weight only, Regular. Never apply bold on top.
Spacing
Tracking +1% (0.012em). In PowerPoint that is "Normal" or at most +1pt. Line spacing 0.92 to 0.95, so lines sit tight.
Colour
White on dark. Ink (#0a0b0f) on light. Mint (#2dd4bf) for the stat numbers.

The script. One line, used sparingly.

Inter

Body copy. Everything people actually read.

Use for
Paragraphs, bullets, captions, buttons, table text, speaker bios, anything longer than a line.
Case
Sentence case. Never all caps in Inter, that job belongs to Bebas Neue.
Weight
Body is Light (300) on the website, which reads beautifully on screen. For slides on a projector step up to Regular (400). Medium (500) for buttons and labels, SemiBold (600) or Bold (700) for emphasis. ExtraBold (800) only on tiny badges like "NEW".
Spacing
Tracking 0. Line spacing 1.5 to 1.7 for paragraphs, 1.4 for short lines.
Colour
On dark: white for the lead line, Slate 200 (#e2e8f0) for body, Slate 400 (#94a3b8) for secondary. On light: Ink (#0a0b0f) and Muted (#718096).

03 · Colour

The palette

Click any swatch to copy its hex code. In PowerPoint pick More Colours, then Custom, and paste it into the Hex box. Keynote: open the colour wheel, choose the sliders tab, and paste into the Hex Colour field. Canva and Google Slides both take hex codes straight into the colour picker.

Core: the three that do the work

Almost every RecConnect slide is Ink, white and Mint. Start there and add nothing until the slide asks for it.

Mint family

Mint is the brand colour. The light and dark versions exist for hover states on the website and for contrast on light backgrounds; on a slide you will mostly use the main Mint.

Darks, greys and text colours

Text on dark backgrounds is never pure grey. Use these exact tints so it matches the website.

Accents: use one at a time

Coral is for "new", urgency and the book promo. Gold is for membership pricing highlights. They are seasoning, not a main course.

CONNECTED 2026 only

The annual summit has its own after-dark look: near-black with one hot orange light. Do not use orange on general RecConnect material, and do not use Mint as the hero colour on CONNECTED material.

04 · Slide sizes

Point sizes that work

The website uses pixels; slides use points. These are tuned for a standard 16:9 widescreen slide (13.33 by 7.5 inches). If your deck is 4:3 or a smaller custom size, scale everything down by about a fifth.

Character spacing in PowerPoint is in points, not percent. The sum is tracking times font size: a 14pt Bebas kicker at +28% needs about 4pt of spacing, an 18pt one needs 5pt.

06 · Whatever you are using

Set up in your app

Install the fonts first (double-click each file, then Install), restart the app, and then set these once so you never have to think about them again.

PowerPoint

Theme fonts: View, Slide Master, Fonts, Customise Fonts. Headings: Anton. Body: Inter.

Theme colours: Slide Master, Colours, Customise Colours. Text/Background Dark 1 is Ink #0A0B0F, Light 1 is #FFFFFF, Accent 1 is Mint #2DD4BF, Accent 2 Coral #FB6B4B, Accent 3 Gold #F2B33D.

Sending the deck on: File, Options, Save, tick "Embed fonts in the file" so it looks right on other machines. Reissue is an OpenType file and some PowerPoint versions will not embed it, so check the deck elsewhere or send a PDF.

Keynote

Install the fonts through Font Book and Keynote sees them immediately. Build a master slide with the Ink background and the hero lockup, then duplicate it.

Hex colours: click the colour well, choose the sliders icon, and paste the hex into the field at the bottom.

Export to PDF embeds every font, Reissue included.

Google Slides

Anton, Bebas Neue, Inter and Space Grotesk are all already in Google's font list: open the font menu, choose More fonts, and search by name.

Reissue is not available in Google Slides. For a script line, set it in Keynote or Canva, export as a transparent PNG and drop it in.

Theme colours: Slide, Edit theme, then the Colours menu accepts hex codes.

Canva

Brand Kit (Canva Pro): upload all five font files under Fonts, add the palette under Colours, and upload the four logos. After that every template snaps to brand.

Canva's letter spacing slider runs from 0 to 1000; the +28% kicker is roughly 280 and the meta lines are about 160 to 220.

07 · House rules

Do this, not that

Do

  • Start every slide on Ink (#0a0b0f) with white text and one Mint accent.
  • Keep Anton in capitals and Reissue in sentence case.
  • Let the headline breathe. Three words on a line is plenty.
  • Use one script line per slide, and make it the payoff, not the title.
  • Darken photos behind text so the white always wins.
  • Use the reversed logo on dark and the colour logo on white.
  • Write with full stops or commas, not dashes. Short sentences. Confident, warm, direct.

Don't

  • Stretch, condense or squash any font manually. They are already the right width.
  • Bold Anton or Bebas Neue. They only come in one weight and faux bold looks smeared.
  • Set paragraphs in Anton or Bebas Neue. Anything longer than a line is Inter.
  • Use pure black (#000000) or default PowerPoint greys. Use Ink, Slate and the text tints here.
  • Put Mint text on a white slide. Use Mint dark (#14b8a6) there instead.
  • Mix Coral, Gold and Orange on the same slide. One accent, and orange is for CONNECTED only.
  • Recolour the logo, add a drop shadow, or put the colour logo on a photo.
