New to this? Step-by-step guide
  1. Copy post: click the Copy post button above. Your words are now saved, ready to paste.
  2. Download image: click Download image. The picture saves to your Downloads folder.
  3. Share to LinkedIn: click the blue button. LinkedIn opens in a new tab.
  4. In LinkedIn, click Start a post (the box near the top that says "Start a post").
  5. Click the photo icon, then pick the image you just downloaded.
  6. Click into the text box and paste: Ctrl + V on Windows, Cmd + V on Mac.
  7. Give it a quick read, then click Post. That's it.
